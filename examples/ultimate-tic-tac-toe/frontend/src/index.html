Ultimate tic-tac-toe — a 3x3 meta-board of nine ordinary tic-tac-toe boards. X moves first. The cell you play sends your opponent to the matching local board next — landing on the board's center cell sends them to the center board, for instance — unless that board is already decided, in which case they may play anywhere. Win a local board with three in a row to claim it on the meta-board; three claimed local boards in a row, column, or diagonal wins the whole match. Your Player ID is per browser tab — open a second tab to play the other seat — unless you log in with Internet Identity, which gives you the same Player ID everywhere, permanently. An idle board can be taken over after 60 seconds.
